Delete your Balance account

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Deleting your account permanently removes it and all data synced to our servers: tasks, goals, sessions, completion history, notes, work days, sprints and settings. It cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Balance and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This needs an internet connection. Data kept only on your device in guest mode is removed when you uninstall the app.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, write to vanrwei@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will delete the account and reply within 30 days.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • If you signed in with Apple, you can also remove Balance under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
