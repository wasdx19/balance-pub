Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Balance is made by Angsar Kairat (“we”, “us”). Balance works without an account, and by default everything you enter stays on your device. This policy explains what leaves your device, when, and why.

1. What we collect

Without an account (guest mode). Your tasks, goals, sessions, notes, work days, sprints and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

If you sign in with Google or Apple. From Google we receive your account ID, email address and, if available, your name and profile picture. From Apple we receive your account ID and the email address you choose to share (a private relay address if you hide your email); Apple provides your name only the first time you sign in, and we store it with your account. Your Balance data (tasks, goals, sessions, completion history, notes, work days, sprints and settings) is synced to our servers so it is available on your other devices and can be restored.

If you generate a goal plan. The goal you describe (its title, details, level, type, sessions per week, session length and duration) is sent to Google Gemini to create the plan. Google may use what is sent and the generated plan to improve its products and AI models, and human reviewers may read them after they are disconnected from your account. Do not include personal or sensitive information in the goal description.

If you buy a subscription. Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. We receive the subscription status, product, purchase date and an anonymous purchase identifier; if you are signed in, it is linked to your account ID.

Device integrity checks. To protect the AI feature from abuse, the app requests a token from Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity that confirms the request comes from a genuine copy of the app.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics: the error and where it happened in the code, device model, system version, app version and random installation identifiers. We do not attach your account to these reports. An error message could, in rare cases, include a fragment of the text the app was processing. This also applies in guest mode.

Notifications. If you turn on the daily wrap-up reminder, it is scheduled on your device. It sends no data to us or to anyone else.

We do not use advertising, ad identifiers, tracking across apps or usage analytics, and we do not sell your data.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store and sync your data, sign you in, and generate goal plans.
  • To manage subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To find and fix crashes.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

For users in the EU/UK, the legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the app and subscriptions) and our legitimate interest in security and keeping the app stable.

4. Storage and retention

On your device, data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. On our servers, synced data is kept while your account exists. Deleted items are first marked as deleted so the deletion reaches all your devices, and are removed together with your account. When you sign out, Balance removes your account data from that device.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all synced data from our servers. You can also request deletion on the account deletion page or by writing to vanrwei@gmail.com. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, write to vanrwei@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

7. Children

Balance is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can access only its own data on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
